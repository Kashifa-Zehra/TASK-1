The Craft

From pod
to perfection

Every step is deliberate. Every decision is purposeful. We take no shortcuts on the path from cacao tree to your hands.

01
🌱

Harvest

Hand-picked at peak ripeness from partner farms. Only pods with the deepest color and richest aroma make the cut.

02
🫘

Ferment

Seven days in wooden boxes, turning twice daily. This is where the flavor precursors are born.

03
⚙️

Grind

72 hours on granite stone rollers. Slow, cool, and constant. No shortcuts. No compromise.

04
✨

Temper

The final dance of temperature and time. A perfectly tempered bar snaps with a sound you can feel in your chest.
